Understanding Our Grading System

We use standardized grades so every product condition is transparent

Grade A

Like New

Minimal to no signs of use. Full functionality. May include original accessories. Cosmetically near-perfect with only micro-scratches invisible to naked eye.

Grade B

Good Condition

Light visible wear — minor scratches or scuffs. Fully functional. May not include original box. Perfect for budget-conscious buyers who don't mind cosmetic imperfections.

Grade C

Fair Condition

Moderate signs of use — visible scratches, dents, or marks. Fully functional. Ideal for buyers who prioritize performance over aesthetics at a great price.
